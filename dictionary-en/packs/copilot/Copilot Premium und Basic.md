Copilot Premium and Basic are the licence tiers that decide what [Microsoft Copilot](./Microsoft Copilot.md) is allowed to do for you. You see the labels in Word, Excel or the Copilot app: “Microsoft 365 Copilot (Premium)”, “Microsoft 365 Copilot (Basic)” and “Copilot Chat (Basic)”. The licence is still called Microsoft 365 Copilot, even though the product has been called Microsoft Copilot since October 2026.

Premium is the paid add-on licence, a [business plan](./Business-Lizenz.md) in the sense of this dictionary, which your company buys per person on top of its Microsoft 365 subscription. With it, Copilot reaches your work data automatically: emails, files, meetings and chats, through [Microsoft Graph](./Microsoft Graph.md) and Work IQ. You get the full Copilot in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and Teams, plus [Researcher and Analyst](./Researcher und Analyst.md), the Agent Builder and priority access: faster answers and reliable availability even at peak times. Agents that work with company data are generally included.

Without the add-on you still have Copilot. [Microsoft Copilot Chat](./Microsoft Copilot Chat.md) comes with the common Microsoft 365 subscriptions and sits under the same protection ([Enterprise Data Protection](./Enterprise Data Protection.md)). The difference is the [grounding](./Work-Grounding und Web-Grounding.md): Copilot Chat answers from the web and from what you hand it yourself, meaning uploaded files or the item you have open in Outlook or Teams. It does not search your mailbox on its own. The label “Microsoft 365 Copilot (Basic)” marks users without the add-on who still see Copilot features in Word, Excel, PowerPoint and OneNote, but with standard access: at peak load a feature can be temporarily unavailable, with no error message. Whether unlicensed users see these features in the apps depends on the tenant. In many larger organisations it stays at Copilot Chat (as of October 2026).

In between sits pay-as-you-go. The company can switch on agents that use work data for unlicensed users and pay by consumption, billed in [Copilot Credits](./Copilot Credits.md). It is off by default and the admin has to set it up.

In practice this means: if you work every day with company emails, minutes and documents, you need Premium. If you mostly draft text, do research and occasionally upload a file, Copilot Chat gets you a long way. The Copilot app shows your tier under your profile.

_Common misconception:_ “Copilot Chat is the free version, so it is not secure.” Enterprise Data Protection applies to every tier as soon as you sign in with your work account. The difference is access to work data, not data protection.

_In practice:_

> “Copilot can't find my emails from last week. It works for you, though.”
>
> “You have Copilot Chat, I have the Premium licence. You have to open or upload the email, mine searches the mailbox by itself.”
