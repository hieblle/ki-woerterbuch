Microsoft Copilot Chat is the AI chat that anyone with a Microsoft 365 work account can use without the company buying a Copilot license. You reach it in the Copilot app, in Teams, in Outlook and in the browser. It answers questions with help from the web, writes and rewrites text, analyses uploaded files and generates images.

The name has changed several times. In 2023 the product was called Bing Chat Enterprise, then for a while simply Copilot, then Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat. Older texts also use “Business Chat” or “BizChat”. Since October 2026 it is Microsoft Copilot Chat. It always means the same thing: the chat you use with your company account.

The main difference from [Microsoft Copilot](./Microsoft Copilot.md) with a license is grounding. Without a license, Copilot Chat answers from the web and from what you give it: an uploaded file, a pasted text, the email currently open in Outlook. It does not search your mailbox, your calendar or SharePoint on its own. With a license, the same chat switches to [work grounding](./Work-Grounding und Web-Grounding.md) and pulls in work data automatically. The interface looks the same, the answers are different.

The second difference is priority. Users without a license have standard access. When the service is busy, features such as file upload, image generation or reasoning models can be temporarily limited. Licensed users have priority access and also get the voice features.

Even so, Copilot Chat is not a toy. It runs under [Enterprise Data Protection](./Enterprise Data Protection.md): prompts and answers are treated like your email, are not used to train the models and are covered by the company's compliance tools. That is why many companies allow Copilot Chat and ban private chatbots. What you type here stays in the company. A personal ChatGPT account offers no such guarantee ([shadow AI](./Schatten-KI.md)).

Agents are available without a license too. Microsoft's coaching agents such as Writing Coach or Prompt Coach, and home-built agents that only work from instructions and the web, are free to use. Agents that access company data can be enabled by the company through usage-based billing ([Copilot Credits](./Copilot Credits.md)). [Copilot Pages](./Copilot Pages.md) and Notebooks are available as well. Admins can pin the chat, switch off web search or remove it entirely.

_Common misconception:_ Many people treat Copilot Chat as a cut-down trial version. It is a full chat with web access and data protection commitments. The only thing it lacks is automatic access to work data.

_In practice:_

> “Can I paste the contract draft into Copilot Chat? I'm not supposed to do that in ChatGPT.”
>
> “With your company account, yes. The green shield shows you Enterprise Data Protection is on, so the text stays with us. Still leave out names you don't need.”
