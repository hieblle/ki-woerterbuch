The Agent Store is the catalogue in Microsoft Copilot where you find ready-made [agents](./Agent.md) and add them to your Copilot. You open it in the Copilot app under Agents and see three kinds of publishers: agents from Microsoft, agents from partners and third parties, and agents that your own company has built and approved.

From Microsoft come, for example, the coaching agents such as Writing Coach or Prompt Coach, the Facilitator for Teams meetings, the Interpreter for live translation, and [Researcher and Analyst](./Researcher und Analyst.md). Partners offer agents that bring their systems into Copilot, for CRM, ticketing or travel booking, say. From your own organisation come agents built in [Copilot Studio](./Copilot Studio.md), submitted by a department and approved by IT, marked “Built by your org”.

What you see in the store is decided by IT. Admins set whether users may add agents from Microsoft, from external publishers or only from the company itself. They can block individual agents, roll them out to everyone or pin them. Before an agent from Copilot Studio appears in the store for the organisation, it goes through a review. There someone checks which data the agent sees and what it may do ([AI governance](./KI-Governance.md)).

The flip side of the store is the Agent Registry. That is the management view in the Microsoft 365 admin center where admins see every agent in the company: from Microsoft, from partners, published by the organisation or shared by individuals. It shows status, publisher, channels and data sources, flags orphaned agents whose creator has left the company. The store is the user view, the registry is the inventory.

For you, day to day, this means: before you build an agent yourself, look in the store. And if your company allows partner agents, read which data an agent accesses before you add it. An agent in the store has been checked for compliance with company rules, not for whether it fits your task.

Without a license: Microsoft's coaching agents and agents that only work from the web and instructions can be used for free with [Microsoft Copilot Chat](./Microsoft Copilot Chat.md). Agents with access to company data need a Copilot license or usage-based billing.

_Common misconception:_ The Agent Store is not an app store with open sign-up. IT decides which agents appear there, and agents from your own organisation only get in after review.

_In practice:_

> “I built an agent in Copilot Studio for our travel expense questions. How do I get it to the team?”
>
> “For a few people, share it directly. If it should be in the Agent Store for the whole company, submit it for publishing and IT approves it. Then it shows up under ‘Built by your org’.”
