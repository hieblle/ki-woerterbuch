title: Business License
description: The company version of an AI service, with a data processing contract, no training on your inputs and admin control

---

A business license is the company version of an AI service, such as ChatGPT Business or Enterprise, Claude for Work, Gemini in Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 Copilot. It differs from the free version less in what the AI can do than in the conditions under which it does it.

The most important difference concerns your inputs. With many free versions, the providers may use what you type to train their models. With business licenses this is excluded by contract. Your draft quote, the customer list or the internal minutes remain your data and will not turn up one day in an answer for somebody else.

The second difference is the contract. A business license usually comes with a data processing agreement (DPA) in which the provider commits to how it handles data, where it is stored and who may access it. Only with that in place can the use be reconciled with the rules under [GDPR and AI](./DSGVO und KI.md). Without this contract, the company generally may not put [personal data](./Personenbezogene Daten.md) into the tool, and whether employees do it anyway with private accounts is something nobody can control.

The third difference is administration. IT can create and block accounts, decide which features and connectors are allowed, and see how heavily the tool is used. That helps with the rollout and with switching access off when someone leaves the company.

This is why the company provides a license instead of saying “just use ChatGPT”. The license bundles data protection, contract and control, and it gives you a clear answer to the question of which tool you may use for company data. The opposite, private accounts for company work, is called [shadow AI](./Schatten-KI.md).

What the license does not do: it does not make the answers more correct. Errors, inventions and gaps stay the same as in the free version, and the [human-in-the-loop](./Human-in-the-Loop.md) principle, meaning a person checks the result, still applies.

_Common misconception:_ “With the business license I can enter anything.” The license governs what the provider does with the data. What you may enter is governed by your [AI policy](./KI-Richtlinie.md), and that can be stricter.

_In practice:_

> “I just use my private ChatGPT for the customer emails, it’s quicker.”
>
> “Better not. With the company’s business license, inputs aren’t used for training and there’s a contract covering it. With your private account you don’t know that.”
