---
title: Prompt
description: Your input to the AI, meaning a question, an instruction or material, from which the answer is produced
aliases: [Input, Instruction, Request]
---

A prompt is everything you give the AI in a single step: a question, an instruction, a pasted text, or a combination of these. The word originally means something like “a cue” or “a request”. In ChatGPT, Microsoft 365 Copilot or Claude, the prompt is simply what you type into the input field and send off.

The answer can only be as good as the prompt. A [language model](./Sprachmodell.md) knows neither your department nor the reason behind your question. It only sees the text in the chat. If you write “Make this shorter” without pasting the text, nothing useful can come of it. If you write “Shorten this email to five sentences and keep the tone friendly” and attach the email, the AI has everything it needs.

A good prompt contains three things. First, the goal: what should exist at the end, a summary, a draft, a list of questions? Second, the [context](./Kontext.md): who is it for, what is it about, what has already happened? Third, the [output format](./Ausgabeformat.md): table or running text, how long, which language, which tone. Not every prompt needs all three parts in detail. For a short translation, one sentence is enough. For a proposal to a new customer, a paragraph of preparation pays off.

Material belongs directly in the prompt. If you want meeting notes summarised, paste the notes or attach the file. In Microsoft 365 Copilot you can reference files from OneDrive or SharePoint directly, and Copilot pulls in the content itself. Whatever is not in the prompt or the attached material does not exist for the AI.

A prompt does not have to be perfect. You can add to it during the conversation and [refine](./Iterieren.md) the answer. For tasks that come up again and again, a [prompt template](./Prompt-Vorlage.md) that you only adapt is worth the effort.

_Common misconception:_ Many people believe a prompt has to be a secret formula or a special command. In fact it is simply a clear work request in full sentences, the kind you would write to a colleague.

_In practice:_

> “Copilot gave me some generic stuff about sustainability for the customer presentation. Completely useless.”
>
> “What was in your prompt? If it just says ‘presentation sustainability’, that is never going to work. Put in which customer, which industry, how many slides, and attach the last proposal.”
