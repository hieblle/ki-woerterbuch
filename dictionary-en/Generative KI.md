---
title: Generative AI
description: AI that creates new content such as text, images, code or audio instead of only sorting or rating what already exists
aliases: [GenAI, Generative KI]
---

Generative AI is a form of [artificial intelligence](./Künstliche Intelligenz.md) that creates new content. Text, images, code, tables, audio or video are produced on request instead of being picked from an existing collection.

The difference from classic analytical AI lies in the output. A spam filter sorts mail into two buckets. A sales forecast delivers a number. Both evaluate something that already exists. Generative AI, by contrast, writes the reply to a customer complaint, drafts an image for a job ad or summarizes meeting minutes. The result did not exist before.

In the office you meet generative AI mostly in chat tools. ChatGPT, Microsoft 365 Copilot, Claude and Gemini are all built on a [language model](./Sprachmodell.md) that produces text. Copilot is also built into Word, Outlook and Teams, where it writes drafts, summaries or suggested replies. Images have their own tools, see [image generation](./Bildgenerierung.md).

Creating content has a downside. Because the model builds content from scratch, it can also build things that are not true, for example a made-up standard or a contact person who never existed. This is called a [hallucination](./Halluzination.md). A spam filter does not invent emails. A language model can. That is why the rule for generative AI at work is: the draft comes from the tool, the checking stays with you.

Typical tasks where generative AI helps are drafts for emails and quotes, summaries of long documents, translations, first versions of job ads or training materials, and rewriting text for a different audience. It is less suitable when exact figures, current facts or legally binding wording matter, unless you give it the sources.

_Common misconception:_ Generative AI does not “look up” the answer in a database. It generates it piece by piece, and it sounds convincing even when the content is wrong.

_In practice:_

> “Can Copilot build me a comparison table from these three quotes?”
>
> “Yes, that's exactly generative AI. You feed in the PDFs, it generates the table. But double-check the prices, it sometimes reads them wrong.”
