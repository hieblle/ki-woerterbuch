---
title: Multimodal
description: Models that can understand or create images, audio or files in addition to text
aliases: [Multimodal model, Multimodality, Vision]
---

A [model](./Modell.md) is called multimodal when it can handle more than one kind of content. It reads not only text but also images, audio, video or files, and some models can create these kinds of content too.

A pure [language model](./Sprachmodell.md) knows only text. A multimodal model is given a photo and describes what is in it, or listens to a recording and writes it down. Technically, image and sound are translated into the same kind of units the model uses to process text. All you see is that the chat window offers a paper clip or a microphone.

In everyday work this is often more practical than typing. You photograph the whiteboard after the meeting and have the points turned into a list. You take a screenshot of an error message and ask what it means. You upload a chart from a presentation and have it explained in three sentences. You speak a note into your phone, and ChatGPT or Gemini turns it into an email. In Microsoft 365 Copilot, much of this falls under working with attached files, for example when Copilot takes notes of a meeting in Teams. More on that under [transcription](./Transkription.md).

The limits lie in accuracy and detail. The model sometimes misreads handwriting on the whiteboard, especially numbers and names. From a photo of a table it does not always get the values into the right column. So an image does not replace the original file. If you have the Excel file, give it the Excel file, not a screenshot of it.

Then there is the data question. A photo of a whiteboard can contain names, customer numbers or prices that you would never type into a chat. The same rules apply to images as to text, that is, your company's [AI policy](./KI-Richtlinie.md) and the usual care with [personal data](./Personenbezogene Daten.md).

_Common misconception:_ “The model can do images” does not automatically mean it can create images. Understanding and creating are separate capabilities, and not every tool offers both.

_In practice:_

> “After the workshop all I have is a photo of the flipchart, and half of it is handwriting.”
>
> “Upload it to Copilot, it's multimodal and will turn it into a list for you. But check the numbers on the flipchart, it tends to misread those.”
